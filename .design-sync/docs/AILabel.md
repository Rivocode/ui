# AILabel

The "IA" badge that marks content generated by artificial intelligence. On its
own, it is a badge; with `explanation`, it becomes a button and opens a panel
saying who generated it, from what data, and what the person should check.
Lives in `@rivocode/ui/ai`.

```tsx
import { AILabel } from '@rivocode/ui/ai'

<AILabel explanation="Escrito pelo assistente a partir das 42 notas de agosto. Confira os valores antes de enviar ao contador." />
```

It is the idea behind Carbon's AI label: whoever reads a summary, a suggested
value or a classification has the right to know it came out of a model, and to
ask how.

## Badge and explanation

Without `explanation`, the badge is text. The screen reader does not hear "I A"
spelled out: it hears "Conteúdo gerado por IA", or whatever `label` says.

With `explanation`, the badge becomes a button with that same name and opens a
`Popover` titled "Gerado por IA" (`title` replaces it) with the explanation
below. `side` decides which side it opens on. The drawing stays the badge's,
20 pixels tall, but the button's touch area extends 8 pixels past it on each
side: the finger does not have to hit a target smaller than the 24 of WCAG
2.5.8.

Write the explanation for whoever is going to decide whether to trust it: the
source ("a partir das notas de agosto"), the limit ("não lê notas canceladas")
and what to check. "Este conteúdo foi gerado por IA" repeats the badge and
explains nothing.

## Tones and sizes

`tone` is `accent` (default) or `neutral`, the same roles as `Badge`; no color
is written in the component. `size` is `sm` (default) or `md`. `text` replaces
the "IA", for another language.

```tsx
<div className="flex items-center gap-2">
  <CardTitle>Resumo de agosto</CardTitle>
  <AILabel tone="neutral" />
</div>
```

## When not to use

- **The status of a record** is `Badge`. "Paga", "Em aberto" and "Cancelada"
  are statuses; `AILabel` states the origin of the content, and only that. A
  `Badge` with the text "IA" loses the spelled-out name for the screen reader
  and the explanation.
- **Content produced entirely in a conversation** needs no badge: the
  `Message` with `role="assistant"` already says who spoke. The badge is for
  what leaves the conversation and goes to live on an ordinary screen.

## In React Native

Translates, on its own path `@rivocode/ui-native/ai`, with the same `text`, `label`, `tone`, `size`, `explanation` and `title`.

**The explanation opens in a `Sheet`.** A panel anchored to the badge would sit under the finger that tapped it, the same reason `Popover` does not port. That is why there is no `side`, and `explanation` is a `string`: it becomes the sheet's description.
